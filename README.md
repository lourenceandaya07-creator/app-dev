# DC’s Legends of Tomorrow

## About the Series

### Overview
**DC’s Legends of Tomorrow** is a *superhero action-adventure* TV series set in the **Arrowverse**.  
It follows a team of heroes and anti-heroes who travel through time to protect history from being altered.

> “Sometimes, being a hero means being a legend.”
